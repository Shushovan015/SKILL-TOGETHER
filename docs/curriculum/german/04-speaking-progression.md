# German Speaking Progression Matrix

| Sublevel | Interaction length | Monologue length | Spontaneous response | Presentation skill | Argumentation / negotiation | Fluency, accuracy, register |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | 2-4 predictable turns with support | 20-30 seconds, rehearsed | Formulaic answers and repair phrases | Name/profile micro-presentation | State preference or choice only | High support; du/Sie and basic politeness. |
| A1.2 | 4-6 turns in routine tasks | 45 seconds on familiar routine | Simple follow-up answers | Describe home, week, or plan | Give one reason | Slow but connected; basic register awareness. |
| A2.1 | 6-8 turns in transactions | 1 minute narrative or explanation | Ask for clarification and alternatives | Explain plan, problem, or comparison | Choose option with reasons | Connected simple speech; emerging accuracy in Perfekt and modals. |
| A2.2 | 8-10 turns across everyday scenarios | 1-2 minutes on experience/recommendation | Handle predictable complications | Short review, biography, or instructions | Recommend and justify | More independent turn-taking; practical politeness. |
| B1.1 | Sustained familiar conversation | 2-3 minutes with sequence and detail | Respond to disruption and counter-question | Plan, summary, or opinion presentation | Support opinion with examples | Threshold fluency; repair and clarification expected. |
| B1.2 | Multi-party familiar discussion | 3 minutes structured talk | Clarify, disagree politely, mediate | Formal/civic/workplace summary | Compare options and propose action | Accurate enough for independence; formal Sie control. |
| B2.1 | Extended discussion with turn management | 4-5 minutes structured professional talk | Build on others and challenge politely | Chart, report, or process presentation | Evidence-based argument | Clear, detailed speech; register and discourse markers controlled. |
| B2.2 | Negotiation and synthesis discussion | 5-6 minutes with Q&A | Handle objections and ambiguity | Professional presentation with audience adaptation | Negotiate compromise and qualify claims | Flexible fluency; nuanced stance and register shifts. |
| C1.1 | Facilitation and complex debate | 7-8 minutes advanced briefing | Infer and respond to implicit meaning | Academic/professional briefing | Critique, concede, and synthesize | Advanced fluency; style shaped by audience. |
| C1.2 | Sensitive discussion and leadership communication | 8-10 minutes polished talk | Diplomatic disagreement and strategic repair | High-stakes presentation or portfolio defense | Negotiate complex tradeoffs | Precise register, idiom control, rhetorical pacing. |
| C2.1 | Demanding public or expert discussion | 10+ minutes under constraints | Rapid, precise response to challenge | Expert briefing or public defense | High-pressure debate and peer critique | Near-native flexibility; subtle stance and voice. |
| C2.2 | Virtually any spoken task | Variable, genre-dependent | Fully spontaneous, nuanced, and exact | Mastery oral portfolio | Expert critique, conflict language, rhetorical play | Mastery-level precision, style, and cultural appropriacy. |
