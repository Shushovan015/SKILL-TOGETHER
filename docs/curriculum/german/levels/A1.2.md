# German A1.2 Curriculum Architecture

This file is architecture, not learner-facing lesson copy. It defines the authoritative module and learning-unit plan for the German track. Daily sessions select activities from these learning units according to the session-length rules in `../13-session-authoring-standard.md`; one learning unit is not equivalent to one daily session.

## Sublevel Contract

- Purpose: stabilize everyday routines and simple service encounters.
- Entry profile: A1.1 introductions, numbers, everyday nouns, present-tense basics.
- Exit profile: Learner can manage routine home, shopping, appointment, leisure, and health interactions with simple connected sentences.
- Skill profile: short connected turns, predictable dialogs, simple notes and messages.
- Module count: 10.
- Learning-unit count: 50.
- Learning-unit fields: the unit ID gives CEFR sublevel and module; each row includes communicative objective, prerequisites/review, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, listening, speaking, reading, writing, interaction, mediation, pragmatic-cultural objectives, real-world task, activity architecture with duration and priority, difficulty, assessment tags, and progression status.

## Module Sequence

| Module | Title | Communicative purpose | Grammar focus | Vocabulary focus | Pronunciation focus | Module evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Everyday schedules and obligations | describe a normal week and simple obligations | modal verbs muessen/koennen as chunks; separable verbs noticing; time phrases | weekly schedule, chores, study/work activities, frequency | word stress in separable verbs; sentence rhythm with time phrases | explain a weekly schedule and one obligation |
| M02 | Home, rooms, and household objects | describe a home and locate objects | definite/indefinite articles review; local prepositions in chunks; kein | rooms, furniture, household objects, location words | r sounds; compound nouns for household items | give a short tour of a room and locate items |
| M03 | Shopping for daily life | compare simple products and ask for help in a store | accusative articles; plural forms; demonstratives as chunks | clothes, household items, sizes, colors, materials | ich/ach ch review; contrastive stress for choices | ask for a product, size, and price |
| M04 | Food planning and invitations | plan a simple meal and invite someone | moechten; gern/lieber/am liebsten; connectors und/aber/denn | meals, ingredients, invitations, acceptance/refusal | oe/ue review; intonation in invitations | invite a partner and agree on a meal plan |
| M05 | Appointments and calendar changes | make, confirm, and change a simple appointment | modal verbs koennen/muessen; time prepositions; sentence-final infinitive noticing | calendar, dates, appointment verbs, availability | month stress; polite intonation | arrange and reschedule an appointment by message |
| M06 | City errands and services | complete routine errands in town | dative in fixed phrases; prepositions zu/bei/nach as chunks | bank, post office, pharmacy, office, service phrases | word boundaries in compounds; final devoicing | ask for a service and follow basic instructions |
| M07 | Free time and simple opinions | talk about leisure activities and reasons | present tense review; gern/lieber; denn and weil as noticing | hobbies, sports, media, places, simple opinion adjectives | sentence stress for opinions; vowel length in hobbies | choose a leisure plan and give one reason |
| M08 | Health and wellbeing basics | describe simple symptoms and ask for help | haben/sein with symptoms; imperative chunks; seit as chunk | body parts, symptoms, remedies, pharmacy phrases | z/ts sound; stress in symptom words | describe symptoms and understand basic advice |
| M09 | Short messages and everyday notices | understand and write practical short messages | word order in short clauses; modal chunks; negation review | SMS/email openings, notices, confirmations, apologies | reading aloud sentence groups; polite formulae | write a short message to confirm, apologize, or ask |
| M10 | A1.2 integrated daily-life project | combine A1.2 language for a week of daily-life tasks | review: modals, accusative, time phrases, connectors, preposition chunks | home, shopping, food, appointments, city, health, messages | review: compounds, polite intonation, vowel contrasts | complete a weekly plan with errands, invitation, appointment change, and message |

## Sublevel Assessment Architecture

- Weekly checks: short retrieval, vocabulary/grammar-in-context, listening or reading detail, and one production item selected from current modules.
- Module assessment: integrated task evidence from U05 plus a focused knowledge check on module grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and pragmatic-cultural conventions.
- Final integrated assessment: combines listening, reading, spoken interaction, written production, mediation, and reflection using the sublevel exit profile.
- Progression decision: learner advances when core module evidence, weekly checks, and final integrated assessment meet the thresholds in `../11-assessment-framework.md`.

## Resource Architecture

- Core resources: model dialogues or texts, vocabulary set, grammar-in-context prompt set, audio script, pronunciation cue, controlled practice bank, production rubric, and real-world task card.
- Optional resources: remedial explanations, alternate input texts, extension prompts, cultural/pragmatic notes, and assessment variants.
