# German A2.1 Curriculum Architecture

This file is architecture, not learner-facing lesson copy. It defines the authoritative module and learning-unit plan for the German track. Daily sessions select activities from these learning units according to the session-length rules in `../13-session-authoring-standard.md`; one learning unit is not equivalent to one daily session.

## Sublevel Contract

- Purpose: move from phrase-level survival to connected transactional language.
- Entry profile: A1 present-tense routines, modal chunks, simple questions, time, prices.
- Exit profile: Learner can plan travel, manage services, describe past events with Perfekt, compare options, and handle routine problems.
- Skill profile: connected simple paragraphs, routine exchanges, short factual input.
- Module count: 10.
- Learning-unit count: 50.
- Learning-unit fields: the unit ID gives CEFR sublevel and module; each row includes communicative objective, prerequisites/review, grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, listening, speaking, reading, writing, interaction, mediation, pragmatic-cultural objectives, real-world task, activity architecture with duration and priority, difficulty, assessment tags, and progression status.

## Module Sequence

| Module | Title | Communicative purpose | Grammar focus | Vocabulary focus | Pronunciation focus | Module evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| M01 | Travel planning and booking | plan a short trip and ask for travel information | Perfekt recognition; comparative chunks; prepositions nach/in/mit | travel, tickets, accommodation, departure/arrival | train-station numbers; unstressed endings | compare two travel options and book one |
| M02 | Changing plans and explaining reasons | change arrangements and explain simple reasons | weil clauses with verb-final order; modal verbs review; Perfekt with haben | delays, reasons, apologies, alternatives, availability | clause-final verb rhythm; polite apology intonation | send a message changing a plan with a reason |
| M03 | Health appointments and advice | make a health appointment and report symptoms | Perfekt with sein/haben; dative pronouns in chunks; imperative advice | doctor, symptoms, medicine, appointment logistics | long compound stress; ach/ich contrast in medical words | book a doctor appointment and summarize symptoms |
| M04 | Housing search and neighborhood | describe housing needs and understand listings | adjective position noticing; es gibt; comparative forms | apartment features, rent, neighborhood, utilities | compound noun stress; schwa endings | select an apartment listing and ask follow-up questions |
| M05 | Past experiences with Perfekt | narrate common past events in simple sequence | Perfekt with regular/irregular participles; haben/sein auxiliaries; time markers | weekend activities, travel events, sequence words | ge- prefix stress; participle endings | tell a short past-event story with three events |
| M06 | Work and learning routines | describe tasks, abilities, and learning progress | koennen/muessen/wollen; dass clauses as noticing; frequency adverbs | work tasks, study habits, skills, progress language | modal sentence rhythm; word stress in abstract nouns | explain current learning/work routine and one goal |
| M07 | Services, complaints, and requests | make a simple complaint and request a solution | dative/accusative in fixed service phrases; negation with nicht/kein; polite subjunctive chunks | service problems, repairs, refunds, customer phrases | polite stress; final consonants in service words | report a service problem and request a repair or refund |
| M08 | Comparing options and making choices | compare options and justify a practical choice | comparative/superlative basics; als/wie; preference structures | prices, quality, distance, features, pros/cons | contrastive stress; comparative endings | compare three options and choose one with reasons |
| M09 | Short factual texts and summaries | read short factual texts and summarize key information | main/subordinate clause recognition; connector review; pronoun reference | public information, simple reports, facts, categories | chunking for reading aloud; pause groups | summarize a short factual text for a partner |
| M10 | A2.1 integrated travel-and-services project | combine A2.1 language for a short trip with a service problem | review: Perfekt, weil, modals, comparison, prepositions, negation | travel, health, housing, services, work/study, factual texts | review: participles, compounds, clause rhythm | plan a trip, change a booking, report a problem, and summarize a notice |

## Sublevel Assessment Architecture

- Weekly checks: short retrieval, vocabulary/grammar-in-context, listening or reading detail, and one production item selected from current modules.
- Module assessment: integrated task evidence from U05 plus a focused knowledge check on module grammar, vocabulary, pronunciation, and pragmatic-cultural conventions.
- Final integrated assessment: combines listening, reading, spoken interaction, written production, mediation, and reflection using the sublevel exit profile.
- Progression decision: learner advances when core module evidence, weekly checks, and final integrated assessment meet the thresholds in `../11-assessment-framework.md`.

## Resource Architecture

- Core resources: model dialogues or texts, vocabulary set, grammar-in-context prompt set, audio script, pronunciation cue, controlled practice bank, production rubric, and real-world task card.
- Optional resources: remedial explanations, alternate input texts, extension prompts, cultural/pragmatic notes, and assessment variants.
