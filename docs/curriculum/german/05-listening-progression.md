# German Listening Progression Matrix

| Sublevel | Input speed | Accent tolerance | Input length | Lexical complexity | Inference target | Note-taking | Follow-up task |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | Very slow, clear, repeated | Standard only | 10-45 seconds | High-frequency chunks | Recognize intention and selected details | Tick boxes, names, numbers | Answer or choose from options. |
| A1.2 | Slow, clear | Standard plus mild variation | 30-90 seconds | Familiar everyday domains | Infer routine context | Short lists and times | Complete message, schedule, or response. |
| A2.1 | Slow-to-natural in routine contexts | Standard and some regional coloring | 1-2 minutes | Routine transactions and problems | Identify reason, sequence, and next step | Key words and action notes | Summarize practical action. |
| A2.2 | Clear natural speech | More speaker variation | 2-3 minutes | Familiar media and public information | Distinguish main idea from examples | Simple outline | Relay requirements or recommendation. |
| B1.1 | Natural clear standard | Several speakers, familiar accents | 3-5 minutes | Familiar work, travel, media topics | Infer attitude in clear speech | Structured notes | Summarize and respond. |
| B1.2 | Natural standard, moderate density | Multiple speakers | 5-7 minutes | Civic, workplace, local issues | Track argument and speaker role | Bullet notes | Mediate rules or meeting outcomes. |
| B2.1 | Extended standard speech | Broad standard variation | 7-12 minutes | Reports, interviews, lectures | Identify argument structure and evidence | Cornell-style or section notes | Produce summary, chart, or memo. |
| B2.2 | Authentic extended input | Regional variation when clear | 10-15 minutes | Dense public/professional topics | Infer stance, hedging, credibility | Source-linked notes | Synthesize across sources. |
| C1.1 | Fast authentic speech | Broad accent tolerance | 15-25 minutes | Abstract, academic, professional | Infer implicit meaning and relationship | Analytical notes | Critique, mediate, or brief. |
| C1.2 | Fast, overlapping, genre-specific | Strong tolerance with occasional replay | 20-30 minutes | Dense idiom and register shifts | Interpret rhetoric, irony, and framing | Selective expert notes | Discourse analysis or negotiation response. |
| C2.1 | Native-speed demanding input | High tolerance including specialized speakers | 30+ minutes | Specialized and implicit language | Track subtext and unresolved ambiguity | High-fidelity synthesis notes | Expert synthesis or crisis briefing. |
| C2.2 | Virtually any input | Very high tolerance | Variable | Full range including humor, allusion, and expert discourse | Interpret layered intent and cultural references | Task-specific expert notes | Publishable summary, critique, or mediation. |
