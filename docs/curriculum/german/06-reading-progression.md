# German Reading Progression Matrix

| Sublevel | Text types | Length range | Vocabulary density | Grammar complexity | Inference target | Strategy | Response task |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | signs, forms, micro-dialogues, menus | 10-80 words | Very low | Simple statements/questions | Match text to immediate need | Scan names, numbers, key words | Fill form or choose response. |
| A1.2 | notices, short messages, simple descriptions | 50-150 words | Low | Present tense, modals, short clauses | Identify purpose and action | Scan and match details | Write confirmation or plan. |
| A2.1 | listings, travel info, simple factual texts | 100-250 words | Low-mid | Perfekt, weil, comparison | Extract sequence, reason, option | Highlight action details | Summarize practical choice. |
| A2.2 | emails, reviews, news briefs, instructions | 150-350 words | Mid | Subordinate clauses, connectors | Identify recommendation and requirement | Outline main/supporting points | Relay instructions or write reply. |
| B1.1 | accessible articles, formal requests, reports | 300-600 words | Mid | Relative clauses, temporal clauses | Distinguish claim and example | Annotate argument sequence | Summary or opinion response. |
| B1.2 | civic/workplace documents, local issue texts | 500-900 words | Mid-high | Passive recognition, formal structures | Infer rules, constraints, stance | Source marking and note extraction | Mediate requirements or write formal response. |
| B2.1 | reports, expert articles, data commentary | 800-1400 words | High | Nominal style, passive, dense noun phrases | Map evidence and argument | Argument map and vocabulary log | Memo, chart explanation, discussion brief. |
| B2.2 | multiple sources, critical media, specialized texts | 1000-1800 words | High | Reported speech, nominalization, hedging | Compare viewpoints and credibility | Cross-source matrix | Synthesis or critique. |
| C1.1 | academic, policy, professional source texts | 1500-2500 words | Very high | Embedded clauses, participial constructions | Evaluate assumptions and implications | Analytical annotation | Critical response or accessible mediation. |
| C1.2 | dense technical, literary, discourse texts | 2000-3500 words | Very high plus idiom | Complex genre-specific syntax | Interpret style, ideology, ambiguity | Genre and discourse analysis | Revision, interpretation, or policy brief. |
| C2.1 | specialized, legal, institutional, expert texts | Variable, demanding | Expert-level | Full formal range | Interpret nuance, gaps, and risk | Expert source triage | Executive synthesis or institutional response. |
| C2.2 | virtually any text, including culturally dense and publishable prose | Variable | Full range | Full range including stylistic play | Interpret layered meaning and rhetorical effect | Purpose-built critical reading | Publishable critique, mediation, or editorial response. |
