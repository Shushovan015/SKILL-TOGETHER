# German Mediation Progression Matrix

Mediation includes relaying, summarizing, paraphrasing, adapting register, and making information usable for another person.

| Sublevel | Source input | Mediation operation | Output form | Pragmatic-cultural demand | Assessment evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1.1 | Names, numbers, signs, micro-dialogues | Relay one detail | Phrase or form field | Basic politeness and du/Sie | Correct transfer of essential detail. |
| A1.2 | Short messages, schedules, simple notices | Relay action, time, or place | Short note or spoken sentence | Apology/invitation conventions | Partner can act on the information. |
| A2.1 | Travel info, service messages, health advice | Summarize practical steps | Bullet list or short message | Polite requests and problem reporting | Required action and reason are clear. |
| A2.2 | Emails, news briefs, instructions | Explain requirement or recommendation | Short summary or reply | Formal email basics, public notice conventions | Accurate main point plus action. |
| B1.1 | Accessible media, workplace updates, forms | Summarize and contextualize | Spoken summary or formal note | Register and recipient adaptation | Main point, support, and next step preserved. |
| B1.2 | Civic rules, meeting notes, local issue texts | Mediate constraints and options | Structured summary or recommendation | Formal/civic expectations | Requirements, deadlines, and options are usable. |
| B2.1 | Reports, charts, interviews | Synthesize evidence for a purpose | Memo, chart summary, oral briefing | Professional precision | Evidence and implications are mapped. |
| B2.2 | Multiple sources and critical media | Compare, synthesize, and qualify | Written synthesis or negotiation brief | Source credibility and stance | Contrasting viewpoints and caveats retained. |
| C1.1 | Specialist or academic source material | Reframe for a non-specialist | Accessible guidance or briefing | Audience adaptation and caveat control | Complexity reduced without distortion. |
| C1.2 | Dense prose, discourse analysis, expert source | Transform register and condense | Multi-register versions or expert note | Diplomacy and rhetorical effect | Register choices are justified. |
| C2.1 | Specialized, institutional, high-pressure input | High-fidelity synthesis under constraint | Executive synthesis, crisis note, interpretation notes | Risk, ambiguity, legal/institutional nuance | Nuance, priority, and risk preserved. |
| C2.2 | Virtually any culturally dense or expert source | Translation-aware mediation and expert reframing | Publishable mediation note or oral defense | Cultural references, implication, style | Meaning, tone, and cultural effect are transferred appropriately. |
