# Product Vision

## Problem

People often create ambitious learning plans and then fail to follow them because every study day requires too many decisions:

- what to learn;
- which resources to trust;
- how long to study;
- what exercises to complete;
- how to prove understanding;
- how to recover after missing a session;
- whether progress is sufficient.

SkillTogether is not primarily a task manager. It removes recurring learning decisions and turns a long programme into clear daily action.

## Vision

SkillTogether gives each learner one clear daily session containing everything needed to learn, practise, verify, and continue. It combines structured curriculum, deterministic scheduling, weekly assessments, recovery support, and partner accountability.

The MVP serves two primary learners:

- a software-engineering learner preparing for practical employment;
- a project-management learner preparing practical delivery artifacts;
- both may additionally follow a shared German Learning Track.

## Product Promise

Open the application, complete today's lesson, prove what you learned, and let the system guide the next session.

## Users

| User | Need |
| --- | --- |
| Software Engineering Learner | Build practical engineering skill, portfolio evidence, and interview readiness. |
| Project Management Learner | Build project artifacts, delivery judgement, and employment readiness. |
| German Learner | Complete short, repeated language sessions with review and assessment. |
| Accountability Partner | See enough progress to support consistency without invading privacy. |
| Content Administrator | Maintain reviewed lessons, resources, and assessment questions. |

## Value Proposition

- Learners know exactly what to do today.
- Lessons contain objectives, explanation, examples, exercises, checks, and approved resources.
- Weekly assessments prove understanding and detect weak topics.
- Missed sessions trigger recovery instead of making the plan feel failed.
- Partners see progress signals, not private notes or exact answers.
- Administrators can improve curriculum safely through versioned content.

## Success Definition

The MVP is successful when pilot learners can configure a Study Plan, complete at least four weeks of scheduled lessons, recover from missed sessions, complete weekly assessments, and share meaningful progress with a partner without needing external planning tools.

Target operating metrics for MVP validation are listed in [02 Product Requirements](02-product-requirements.md).

## Principles

- Default to clarity over feature volume.
- Make today's work the primary dashboard focus.
- Prefer deterministic rules for scheduling and assessment eligibility.
- Preserve completed historical results even when content changes.
- Use supportive recovery language and avoid guilt-based design.
- Keep privacy boundaries explicit.
- Treat AI as assistance, not authority.

## Non-Goals

- SkillTogether is not a public course marketplace.
- SkillTogether is not a social network.
- SkillTogether is not a video-hosting platform.
- SkillTogether is not a calendar replacement.
- SkillTogether will not use AI as the sole curriculum author, scheduler, authorization decision-maker, or production grader for objective questions.
